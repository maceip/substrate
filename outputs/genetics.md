# The Genetics Problem (Jun 12 2026)

The user's framing: our roots — the mined corpora — may lack the right agentic "genetics."
Two failure modes follow: (1) we build something that misses what users in the agent era
actually want, and (2) the system collapses into complexity. Both are real. This is the
analysis and the standing mitigations.

## THE GOVERNING INSIGHT (user, Jun 12): this is not a third problem

If the four papers were integrated correctly — metabolism, not just anatomy — the genetics
problem would not exist as a META-problem, because each paper is exactly a mechanism for
making inheritance non-fatal: FoT rewrites the genome from usage; AEvo tightens away wrong
genes without being asked; MOSS makes the source itself mutable under a protected evaluator;
Meta-Agent attributes a miss to the gene that caused it. Wrong genetics in a closed-loop
system is a WORKLOAD (failures → attribution → lessons → tightenings → rewrites). Wrong
genetics in a half-loop system is a WORRY, carried by the human. Every mitigation below is
a human manually performing what a closed loop would do by itself — which means the real
fix for this document is the closing-moves list in four-papers-map.md, and this document
should shrink as those moves land. Its length is a measure of the gap.

## The diagnosis is empirically confirmed

Every agentic operation in the 215-block catalog traces to ONE source: the user's own repos
(vet, runcards, cordon, cursor-anchor, tenet). The entire mainstream corpus — 16 product
repos from ERPNext to Mattermost — contributed zero agent-shaped verbs, because open-source
agentic *products* with mature, mineable invariants barely exist yet. Our genes are
2015-2024 SaaS patterns plus one thin strand of frontier agent work.

## Why this is less fatal than it looks: the genes are the classes, not the clusters

The catalog's deep finding is that all ~225 mined ops collapse into four invariant classes
(conservation · fail-closed-verify · events→verdict · canonical-formula). Those classes are
substrate-independent: an agent-native product needs spend-once, entitlement gates, audit
logs, and reproducible verdicts MORE than a CRUD app does, because autonomous actors make
every conservation and verification failure worse. The perishable part of the catalog is the
cluster surface (CRM stages, funnels, dunning); the durable part is the gate library. If the
agent era invalidates clusters, the classes survive — and the classes are what the built
machinery (gates, grades, protection) actually runs on.

## The honest residual risk and the hedge

Nobody knows which agent-native product patterns will crystallize; there is no corpus to
mine because the world hasn't written it yet. The hedge is the strata rule applied forward:
- WHERE A PATTERN EXISTS, wrap it (we did this for validation with zod).
- WHERE NONE EXISTS, the user's own frontier repos ARE the strata — hand-rolling and FoT
  lesson-accumulation are justified there and only there. This is also the competitive
  asymmetry: our weirdest genes are the ones nobody else has.
- GENETICS GET EDITED BY USAGE, not fixed at birth: HANDROLLED markers, friction reports,
  and repeats from real projects are the mutation mechanism. The blind test proved usage
  signal beats mining signal; the sunset clause already retired mining as a mode. When
  agent-native OSS products appear, mine them then.
- The agentic-layer mine (Jun 12, in flight over vet/runcards/cordon/cursor-anchor/pinback)
  adds the missing strand from the only available source.

## The complexity-collapse dimension

The system itself is the second place bad genetics show up: every mechanism added (gates,
grades, lens, steward, FoT, catalog) carries maintenance duties, and duties without
metabolism land on the human, and humans quit. Standing defenses, now constitutional:

1. **User lens holds veto** — a mechanism that doesn't show up in felt experience stops growing
   (one-proof-artifact rule).
2. **Complexity budget**: no new mechanism without naming what it retires or chokepoints.
   The sunset clause (mining → usage signal) is the precedent; the steward absorbing sync/
   update/lens duties is the second.
3. **Generated views over hand-maintained prose** (CATALOG.md, insights.md sections) — docs
   that cannot drift because they are outputs, not sources.
4. **The repeat ledger as the tripwire** — complexity collapse manifests as the same problems
   recurring; the ledger makes that a number with a target of zero.

## What would change this assessment

If, after 2-3 real projects, the HANDROLLED markers cluster in agent-task territory the
catalog cannot serve, the genetics critique is confirmed in practice and the agentic corpus
becomes the build priority regardless of the door. If instead markers cluster in classic
domains, the SaaS genes were the right inheritance and the worry was premature. Either way
the instrument that decides is usage, not opinion.

## CRISPR (user, Jun 12): the name for the missing coupling

The components all exist, disconnected: guide RNA = attribution + repeat ledger + HANDROLLED
markers (targeting); Cas9 = agent source-rewrite (today human-triggered); repair template =
strata rule + federated lessons (never freestyle); PAM safety = the protected evaluator
(no cut where verification is absent); germline vs somatic = nursery edit propagated by the
update channel vs app-level handroll; and the spacer archive (CRISPR's original immune
memory) = the parked federated-cases design. CRISPR v1 is one coupled loop: failure →
attribute the block → draft repair from template → apply in nursery → suite green →
[auto-tighten] commit → update channel propagates. Somatic edits free; germline edits
human-gated until trust accumulates. The five closing moves in four-papers-map.md are this
assembly, in order.
